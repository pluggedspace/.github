## What We Do

We combine **research, engineering, and deployment** to turn difficult technical problems into systems that can operate in the real world.

Our work spans:

* Intelligent software
* Data and intelligence infrastructure
* Automation and agentic systems
* Software infrastructure
* Security and governance
* Emerging technologies

The specific technologies we work on will change. **The problems we choose to solve are the constant.**

## How We Work

Our work follows a simple cycle:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Investigate] --> B[Build]
    B --> C[Test]
    C --> D[Measure]
    D --> E[Learn]
    E --> A
```

Research informs engineering.

Engineering produces systems.

Systems generate evidence.

Evidence informs what we build next.

Some work becomes a product. Some becomes infrastructure. Some becomes open source. Some is archived.

**Not everything needs to become a product.**
